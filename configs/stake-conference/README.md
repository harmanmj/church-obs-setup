This Scene Collection is intended for Stake Conference where you want to have the ability to overlay graphics generated by another program and output over NDI. 
It is based upon the dual-camera.json config as it is primarily for use at the Independence building. 

## ATEM Mini

Both Stake Conference collections (NDI and RTSP) have a **Blackmagic** scene, at the bottom of the scene list, for a Blackmagic ATEM Mini plugged into the OBS computer by USB. The scene shows the "ATEM Mini" video capture source, with sound from the "USB Audio" source. The ATEM Mini's own USB audio is muted.

The setup scripts don't choose the capture device. On each computer that uses it, plug in the ATEM Mini, open OBS, double-click the **ATEM Mini** source, and pick the ATEM Mini as the **Device** (it shows as "Blackmagic Design").
